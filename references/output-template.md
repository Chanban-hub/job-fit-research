# Output template

Use the smallest format that remains readable. Avoid a wide table when explanations are long.

## Header

```text
Checked: YYYY-MM-DD, timezone
Scope: graduation year, batch, employment type, cities, ownership, role filters
Profile: source path or conversation-derived
Defaults/limitations: ...
Source depth: quick / standard / deep
```

## Executive recommendation

State:

- the best-fit role families;
- the strongest jobs by absolute quality, even when they are stretch applications;
- the best two or three immediate applications;
- any major mismatch between requested filters and current openings;
- deadline urgency.

## Ranked shortlist

Start with a compact table:

| Rank | Company / role | Type | Status / deadline | Fit | Job quality | Application value | Intensity | Recommendation |
|---:|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Then give one compact block per role:

```text
### Company — exact role

- Status: Open—verified / Likely open / Pipeline / Unverified
- Eligibility: met, unclear, or failed items
- Work: 2–4 plain-language duties
- Fit: NN/100 — decisive matching evidence
- Job quality: NN/100 (confidence) — benchmark; strongest positive and negative drivers
- Application value: NN/100 — why this should or should not receive application effort
- Gap: the largest resume or interview risk
- Intensity: N/5 (confidence) — known facts; inference
- Compensation: range or unknown (Official/Comparable/Reported/Estimated)
- Salary growth: 起薪 → 2–3 年 → 3–5 年，每段标注证据等级（Official/Reported/Market estimate/Inference/Unknown）
- Career development: 2–3 年学习与所有权；3–5 年晋升与外部出口；天花板/锁定与抗风险
- Advice: priority / apply / conditional / stretch / skip
- Sources: direct role page; separate compensation source if used
- 公众号信源: official recruitment account name + certification subject + latest post date, or "none found"
- Source coverage: vacancy / compensation / workload / ownership / stability / exits
- Contradictions: material disagreements or "none found"
```

每个岗位块必须同时包含 `Salary growth` 与 `Career development` 两行；缺任一行视为报告不完整。

## Source coverage

For standard searches, give a compact per-role summary:

| Role | Official vacancy | Official WeChat account | Compensation | Workload | Ownership/stability | Exit evidence | Overall confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

For deep searches, include the claim-level evidence ledger from [source-playbook.md](source-playbook.md) for the top roles. List sources checked but rejected when their exclusion materially changes the conclusion.

## Portfolio strategy

Group roles into:

- priority: strong fit, good job quality, and verified opening;
- realistic: credible chance with manageable gaps;
- stretch: objectively strong job but weaker current fit or high competition;
- backup: realistic but lower absolute quality or weaker alignment;
- avoid: high fit but poor job quality, severe risk, or unacceptable tradeoffs.

Do not place an ineligible role in any application group.

## Next 48 hours

Give concrete actions:

1. deadline-near applications;
2. resume variant or portfolio artifact for the top role family;
3. missing information to verify with recruiter or employee;
4. interview preparation tied to actual duties.

## Evidence labels

Use these exact phrases consistently:

- `Official`
- `Official ATS`
- `Official WeChat account`
- `Regulator/filing`
- `Government employment platform`
- `University/government mirror`
- `Current platform listing`
- `Structured compensation database`
- `Employee report`
- `Social anecdote`
- `Market estimate`
- `Inference`

Place citations or direct URLs near the claims they support. Do not collect uncited claims into a confident-looking summary.

## No-result handling

If a requested category or company has no verified result:

```text
No verified current opening found for <scope> as of <date>.
Checked: <official portals and query scope>.
Closest route: <only a currently actionable pipeline with real dates>, clearly labeled.
```

Never fill a quota with expired or irrelevant roles.
Do not write “预计 X 月开放” or any other not-yet-open window in the report; those roles stay out of the report until they are actually open.
