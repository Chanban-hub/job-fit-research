## 1. Core principle

Evaluate sources per claim, not per website.

- An employer career page is authoritative for duties and deadline, but weak for actual overtime.
- An employee review can inform workload, but cannot prove the role is open.
- A regulator filing can establish ownership and business health, but not team culture.
- A compensation community can provide ranges, but not guaranteed pay for the specific candidate.

Never use one source to support claims it does not directly address.

Report only roles that are currently actionable: open, in an active application window, or a conversion pipeline with dates. Roles that are not yet open (only “expected in month X”) or already expired/closed must not appear in the report body or ranking; keep them in an internal watchlist at most.

## 2. Source-depth requirements

### Quick

- one current employer role page, official ATS, or authoritative government/university mirror;
- eligibility and deadline checked;
- unsupported salary, workload, and prospects left unknown or low confidence.

### Standard

- official vacancy anchor where accessible;
- at least two independent job-quality source families;
- exact city, role family, level, and evidence dates recorded;
- material contradictions disclosed.

### Deep

- official vacancy or campaign plus role-level evidence;
- ownership/legal entity verified when relevant;
- compensation supported by official data or at least two comparable observations;
- workload supported by role facts plus at least two independent, recent, team/city-relevant signals where available;
- business health checked with filings, regulator information, or authoritative reporting;
- exits checked through adjacent job demand or multiple public career trajectories;
- at least four independent source families overall.

Use standard depth for all shortlisted roles and deep depth for the final top three unless the user requests otherwise.

## 3. Vacancy and eligibility sources

Prefer:

1. employer career site and the employer-specific ATS reached from it;
2. company's official recruitment WeChat official account (公众号, certified to the company or its HR subsidiary, with a dated post, role ID/deadline and an application entry);
3. employer-specific ATS or mobile application route linked from that official account;
4. government employment platform;
5. university career center on an official university domain;
6. current reputable job platform;
7. recruiter post with verifiable employer identity;
8. aggregator or social repost for discovery only.

Common ATS families include Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle Recruiting Cloud, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Avature, Phenom, Moka, Beisen/Zhiye and employer-operated e-recruiting systems.

Do not trust an ATS domain merely because the vendor is legitimate. Confirm at least one:

- the employer's official site links to the tenant;
- the tenant shows the correct legal entity, branding and privacy notice;
- an official employer announcement links to the exact tenant.

招聘简章规则（硬规则）：

- 招聘简章/宣传公告只用于了解岗位类别、培养体系和宣传口径，不构成“当前开放”或“某城市有岗”的岗位级证据。
- 简章中的城市列表是计划/可选范围，不是岗位级事实；必须到官网 ATS 或官方招聘公众号菜单的实际投递列表中查到对应具体职位页，才能声称“该城市有该岗位在招”。
- 官网 ATS 无法打开、查不到具体岗位、或只看到简章转载时，状态至多标 `Likely open` 或 `Unverified`，禁止标 `Open—verified`。
- 对每个入围岗位，记录“岗位级锚点来源”：官方 ATS 职位 URL 或官方公众号投递入口；没有岗位级锚点的条目不得进入已核验清单。

Check the actual role page for:

- exact title, city and employing entity;
- graduation window, degree, major and language;
- posting date and deadline;
- full-time versus internship;
- application button or current application route;
- role ID for deduplication.

### WeChat official-account sources (公众号信源)

Treat certified employer recruitment accounts as a first-class vacancy source alongside the career site, and give them a higher share than ordinary aggregators in every search.

Authoritative types, in order:

1. employer's own recruitment account (e.g., `<company>招聘`, `Join<company>`), certified to the legal entity or its HR subsidiary, with a working campus/ATS menu entry;
2. group or business-unit official account carrying dated recruitment posts;
3. government or university employment official accounts (国聘、ncss.cn、高校就业公众号) that republish the employer announcement;
4. verified recruiter or HR public account only as discovery.

How to find and open them:

- search 微信搜一搜 or 搜狗微信搜索 for `<company> 招聘 公众号`, or take the account name from the employer's careers page;
- open the latest dated posts, the campus-recruitment menu, and the embedded ATS/application link;
- record account name, certification subject, post date, role ID, deadline and application route.

Verification rules:

- an official-account post is `Official WeChat account` evidence: authority 3 when certified and dated, 2 when certified but undated, 1 for a repost or unverified account;
- one original post republished by many accounts is one evidence chain, not multiple sources;
- if the account conflicts with the career site or ATS, trust the usable application route plus the newer dated announcement, and disclose the conflict;
- use official-account salary claims as official marketing claims, not guaranteed compensation;
- official-account culture posts do not prove WLB; use role facts plus independent employee reports for workload.

Report the account name and latest post date in the per-role source line and include it in the source-coverage summary.

## 4. China campus and public-sector sources

Use these as authoritative or strong mirrors, while still preferring the employer's own application page:

- 国家大学生就业服务平台 (`ncss.cn`);
- 中国公共招聘网 and高校毕业生服务专区 (`job.mohrss.gov.cn`);
- 人力资源社会保障部高校毕业生招聘专栏 (`mohrss.gov.cn`);
- 国务院国资委人事招聘栏目 and central-SOE announcements (`sasac.gov.cn`);
- 中国人力资源市场网 and中国国家人才网;
- current “国聘行动” participating platforms, including 国聘 and中智招聘;
- provincial and municipal human-resources/social-security bureau portals;
- ministry, research institute and public-institution recruitment pages;
- official university career centers on `.edu.cn` or verified university domains.

Treat “国聘行动” and public-platform listings as curated distribution, not automatic proof that every downstream listing remains open. Re-open the employer application route when possible.

For central SOEs:

- verify the parent in the current SASAC central-enterprise directory;
- verify the employing subsidiary and labor-contract entity separately;
- distinguish direct employment, subsidiary employment, staffing dispatch and project outsourcing.

For public institutions:

- prefer the competent ministry, institution, local human-resources bureau, or official examination portal;
- inspect establishment status, headcount type, written-exam rules and eligible-graduate definition;
- do not equate a government-affiliated employer with an established public-institution position.

## 5. Foreign-company and global sources

Prefer:

- global or China employer careers site;
- employer-specific ATS linked by the official site;
- official early-careers, graduate-program or internship-program pages;
- official local legal-entity pages and verified employer social accounts;
- LinkedIn company Jobs page as a current secondary source;
- official university/Handshake postings as mirrors where accessible.

Search both English and local-language titles. Foreign employers may use:

- graduate program;
- early careers;
- new college graduate;
- university hire;
- working student;
- off-cycle intern;
- conversion internship;
- rotational program.

Verify whether the role is hired by the China entity, an overseas entity, a vendor, or an outsourcing partner. Check work authorization, language, location and conversion wording explicitly.

For global pages, confirm that the role is still listed in the selected country after opening it. Search-engine snippets often retain expired roles.

## 6. Ownership and legal-entity sources

Use:

- 国家企业信用信息公示系统;
- SASAC central-enterprise directory and local SASAC disclosures;
- audited annual reports, prospectuses and issuer announcements;
- Shanghai, Shenzhen, Beijing and Hong Kong exchange disclosures;
- SEC EDGAR or the relevant overseas securities regulator for listed foreign issuers;
- company investor-relations pages;
- official credit, bankruptcy, enforcement or regulator notices where relevant.

Use Tianyancha, Qichacha and similar business databases for discovery and cross-checking, not as the sole source for a decisive ownership claim when a primary registry or filing is available.

Do not infer “state-owned,” “central SOE,” “foreign company,” or “joint venture” from branding. Verify the employing entity, controlling shareholder and current ownership date.

## 7. Compensation and benefits sources

Use in descending claim strength:

1. current official role salary range or official graduate-program package;
2. user-provided written offer or official benefits document;
3. current same-company, same-city, same-level comparable role;
4. multiple dated offer reports for the same company, role family, city and degree tier;
5. structured compensation databases;
6. employee-review and job platforms;
7. broad city/industry estimate.

Useful secondary families include:

- Levels.fyi for technology and multinational compensation;
- Glassdoor, Indeed, Kununu and AmbitionBox for multinational/local comparisons;
- 牛客、OfferShow、看准、BOSS直聘 and verified campus-offer discussions;
- 脉脉 for discovery and recent organizational signals.

Do not treat these communities as equally representative. Record sample size, year, city, level and business unit when available.

Cross-check:

- monthly base and number of salary months;
- guaranteed versus target bonus;
- sign-on, stock and vesting;
- housing, meal, transport and relocation benefits;
- social insurance and housing fund contribution basis;
- probation salary;
- internship pay versus graduate pay.

One anonymous offer is a data point, not a range. Prefer at least three comparable observations; use two only when they are consistent and recent.

Official-recruitment WeChat-account package claims count as official marketing material: record them with the post date and label `Official marketing claim`, then cross-check with comparable offers; never treat a marketing headline as a guaranteed package.

## 8. Workload and culture sources

Start with role-specific facts:

- shift, on-call, travel and customer-site requirements;
- sales quota, incident duty or launch responsibility;
- explicit office hours, hybrid policy and overtime language;
- delivery cadence and global time-zone coordination.

Then triangulate with:

- current employee reviews on Glassdoor, Indeed, Kununu, AmbitionBox or 看准;
- recent team/city/business-unit discussions on 牛客 or 脉脉;
- verified employee or alumni accounts;
- labor-arbitration, regulator or court records for serious recurring issues;
- official work-policy or collective-agreement documents.

Use Reddit, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, Blind and other social posts only as leads or low-confidence anecdotes unless identity, team, city and date are verifiable.

Prefer at least two independent recent reports. Separate:

- company-wide culture;
- business-unit pattern;
- specific team reality;
- temporary launch or restructuring period.

Do not translate “fast-paced,” “ownership,” or “entrepreneurial” directly into an overtime score.

Official WeChat-account culture posts are marketing material and do not prove WLB; rely on role facts plus independent employee reports.

## 9. Business stability sources

Use:

- audited annual and interim reports;
- exchange filings and earnings releases;
- investor-relations presentations and earnings-call transcripts;
- SASAC, ministry or regulator disclosures;
- authoritative reporting on layoffs, plant closures, financing or restructuring;
- official tender, customer or product information when business-unit relevance matters.

Evaluate the employing business unit, not only the group. A profitable parent can contain a declining unit; a loss-making growth unit can still offer strong learning with higher risk.

Check:

- revenue and profit direction;
- cash, debt and funding runway where relevant;
- business-unit strategic importance;
- hiring freezes, layoffs or repeated reorganizations;
- customer concentration;
- policy, export-control, real-estate or commodity-cycle exposure;
- outsourcing and automation risk for the role.

Do not use stock-price movement alone as proof of job stability.

## 10. Career-exit and mobility sources

Use:

- requirements in current adjacent roles across multiple employers;
- multiple public alumni career trajectories on LinkedIn or official alumni pages;
- internal mobility or graduate-rotation program documentation;
- professional certifications and widely used technology/process standards;
- industry reports describing durable demand.

Look for:

- skills learned that appear in external job descriptions;
- ownership of measurable outcomes;
- customer, product, data or regulatory exposure;
- promotion ladder and role clarity;
- portability across industries and cities;
- lock-in to proprietary systems, administrative coordination or one narrow client.

Do not infer strong exits from employer brand alone. Do not publish identifiable personal details from alumni profiles; summarize patterns.

## 11. Claim-level source scoring

Score each source-claim pair out of 10:

| Dimension | Points | Guide |
|---|---:|---|
| authority | 0–3 | primary employer/regulator = 3; authoritative mirror = 2; community/aggregator = 0–1 |
| directness | 0–3 | directly states the claim = 3; close comparable = 2; broad signal = 1 |
| recency | 0–2 | current cycle/current year = 2; one or two cycles old = 1; older/undated = 0 |
| specificity | 0–2 | same entity, role, city and level = 2; partial match = 1; company/industry-wide = 0 |

Apply penalties:

- search snippet only: −2;
- undated screenshot or copied text without provenance: −2;
- obvious SEO/AI aggregation: −2;
- sponsored content not disclosed: −1;
- circular repost that adds no independent evidence: count once.

Interpretation:

- 9–10: high-confidence direct evidence;
- 7–8: medium-high;
- 5–6: medium;
- 3–4: low;
- 0–2: discovery only.

Do not average contradictory sources without explanation.

## 12. Triangulation and contradiction handling

Independence matters more than count.

- A university post copied from an employer announcement and ten aggregators copying it are one evidence chain.
- Two employee reviews repeating the same viral post are not independent.
- Official salary and employee-reported realized bonus address different claims; they may both be correct.

When sources disagree:

1. compare employing entity, team, city, level and date;
2. distinguish guaranteed, target and realized values;
3. prefer direct and recent evidence;
4. report a range or scenario rather than selecting a convenient number;
5. lower confidence;
6. list the unresolved contradiction in the final answer.

If an official page contradicts a current application state, test the application route or mark `Likely open`, not `Open—verified`.

## 13. Search sequence

For each company or role:

1. **Discover:** locate campaign names, role IDs, title variants, ATS domain and official recruitment WeChat official account name.
2. **WeChat anchor:** search (微信搜一搜/搜狗微信搜索) and open the official account's latest dated post, campus menu and embedded application entry; record account name, certification subject and post date.
3. **Anchor:** open the employer role page or highest-authority mirror.
4. **Verify:** confirm entity, eligibility, city, dates and application route.
5. **Broaden:** collect claim-specific compensation, workload, ownership and stability evidence.
6. **Challenge:** search for closure, dispatch, restructuring, pay or workload contradictions.
7. **Compare:** benchmark against same-city and same-role graduate alternatives.
8. **Freeze:** record checked date, URLs, official-account evidence, source scores and remaining uncertainty.

Use targeted queries such as:

```text
<company> <role> <city> 校招 薪资 offer
<company> <business unit> 加班 出差 值班
<company> annual report <business unit>
<company> graduate program salary <country>
site:linkedin.com/jobs <company> <role> <city>
site:edu.cn <company> <graduation year> 校园招聘
site:sasac.gov.cn <company> 招聘
site:mohrss.gov.cn <company> 招聘
```

Search negative evidence deliberately, but do not assume a negative query proves a negative conclusion.

## 14. Low-trust and prohibited shortcuts

Treat as discovery-only unless independently verified:

- SEO job aggregators with no original role URL;
- 招聘简章/宣传公告中无具体岗位 ID 或职位页链接的城市与岗位清单；
- 社交平台“城市神仙外企/福利榜单”：无岗位级锚点，常混入同名本地公司、销售办事处或已撤并实体；
- AI-generated career articles;
- cached snippets for expired pages;
- QR-code posters with no verifiable publisher;
- recruiter accounts with no employer identity;
- salary calculators lacking company/role samples;
- undated screenshots;
- “内部消息” or “保offer” claims;
- training-placement advertisements presented as employment.

Never:

- cite a search-results page as the final vacancy source;
- fabricate access to a login-only page;
- state that an application button works if it was not checked;
- copy personal employee information into the report;
- pay, register, upload sensitive documents or contact recruiters without user authorization.

## 15. Evidence-ledger format

Maintain this structure internally or in a saved snapshot:

| Claim | Conclusion | Source | Family | Date | Score /10 | Independent chain | Conflict or gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| vacancy | Open through YYYY-MM-DD | URL | employer ATS | YYYY-MM-DD | 10 | A | none |
| vacancy | Open through YYYY-MM-DD | 公众号推文 URL | Official WeChat account | YYYY-MM-DD | 9 | A2 | 与官网 ATS 一致 |
| salary | Estimated range | URL | comparable offers | YYYY-MM-DD | 7 | B | bonus months vary |
| workload | Intensity 3/5 | URL | role facts/reviews | YYYY-MM-DD | 6 | C | team unknown |
| ownership | central-SOE subsidiary | URL | SASAC/filing | YYYY-MM-DD | 9 | D | contract entity unclear |

For chat answers, show a compact source-coverage summary. For `source_depth=deep` or saved reports, include the ledger for the top roles.
